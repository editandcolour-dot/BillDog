|---|---|---|---|

| Madibeng (Brits) | Jan 2026 | Jun 2026 | UNKNOWN on all 2024/25 electricity |

Source: Witness (Jan 2026), NERSA notice (10 April 2026)

**Implication:** If a customer was overcharged under the invalid determination, they may be entitled to a credit. If undercharged, they may owe more. Neither scenario is Billdog-actionable until NERSA publishes revised determinations.

**Action required:** Monitor NERSA website for redetermination publication. Once published, update JSON files and re-enable verifications for these municipalities.

## Water Fixed Charge Legal Challenge (CoCT, 2025/26)

SAPOA has challenged CoCT's new property-value-based water fixed charge as potentially unconstitutional — departing from cost-reflectivity principles in the Municipal Systems Act. Case ongoing.

This does NOT invalidate the charge for Billdog purposes — it's still being collected and billed correctly at the gazzetted rate. Flag in dispute documentation if applicable.
